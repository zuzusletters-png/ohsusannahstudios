About Zuzu

I believe there is still magic in the world.

I was nicknamed Zuzu after Zuzu Bailey in It’s A Wonderful Life — the youngest daughter of George Bailey, who believed that every time a bell rings an angel gets its wings. I watch the movie at least once a year, and sometimes more.

I entered this world programmed to love music, horses, performing and people. My mother told me I sang before I talked. I spent many years as a singer, dancer, puppeteer, actor, equestrian and manager for some of the biggest entertainment companies in the world.

It was loss that sent me on a journey of self-discovery and led me to my real purpose: working in equine assisted activities and therapies, helping people heal through partnering with horses. When people ask if I miss performing, I can honestly say no.

It seems to me our world needs a little more love and kindness these days. That is what I hope you find each time you open one of my letters.
