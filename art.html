About the stones

  • Most are between 2 and 3 inches across, and some are a little larger or smaller.
  • Painted by hand in acrylic paint on natural stones, then finished with layers of UV resistant clear coat for a glossy shine.
  • Not every stone is round, flat, or smooth. Because each one is painted by hand, there may be small imperfections, air bubbles, and bumps or divots in the paint.

Caring for your stone

  • They are happiest indoors. Sun and moisture can fade or crack the paint over time.
  • To clean, gently wipe with a damp cloth.

Shipping

  • Each stone is wrapped on its own for protection and packed with recycled materials.
  • Stones ship within the United States only.
  • Shipping is $6.99 for up to 4 stones, and $1 more for each stone after the fourth.
