5 October 2026

One small moment worth remembering.

It has been a month and a half and I now have nine subscribers!! I am working on the second letter that goes out the week of the 19th! Here is how things have evolved so far. September letters had 2 one-time, 1 month-to-month, 1 six-month, 3 twelve-month subscriptions, plus one very special gift letter. A total of 8 separate individuals. October has 1 one-time, 2 month-to-month, 1 six-month, and 4 twelve-month subscriptions, plus one very special gift letter. A total of 9 separate individuals, and there is still time to join before October’s sign-up closes on the 15th! Woohoo!

One of the things that makes me most happy is that, after receiving their letter, one of the single letter subscribers from September bought a year!! It feels so wonderful to think that that subscriber enjoyed their letter so much that they bought an entire year of letters!

I love creating what goes into a letter! It brings me such joy imagining how the letter will be received, and the hope that each reader will create an experience for themselves filled with tranquility. I love imagining the magic of slowing down to experience a moment, and the delight that lasts long after the letter is put away.

Focusing on the Good

Last time, I wrote about hard days. This time, I want to remember the good ones too. Tonight, before bed, write down one thing from today that you want to remember. It does not have to be big. Sometimes the smallest moments are the ones that hold the most magic.

What is one small moment you want to remember from today? I would love to hear it. Send me a note at hello@ohsusannahstudios.com.

Peace and Love,Zuzu
